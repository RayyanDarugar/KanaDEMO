Kana vs. a traditional CDP

Traditional CDPs were built for digital profiles and email lists. Kana is built warehouse-native — directly on your Snowflake or Databricks data, with no duplication, no months-long implementation, and no data leaving your environment.

Traditional CDP

Rigid & Slow
  • ✕
    Siloed customer databases Traditional CDPs only store basic profile identities and events, ignoring POS velocity and inventory schemas.
  • ✕
    Duplicative data storage Requires copying huge lists of data to their warehouse and yours, leading to synchronization drift and security holes.
  • ✕
    6-9 month implementation Requires manual engineering pipelines and rigid pre-defined layouts before you can sync a single segment.
  • ✕
    Real-time limitations Rate limits and strict payload structures prevent campaigns from triggering dynamically off regional POS fluctuations.

Kana Warehouse-Native Platform

Warehouse Native
  • ✓

  • ✓
    Single source-of-truth Keeps ad networks, CRM, and dashboards synchronized by acting directly on your central data cloud.
  • ✓
    Setup in days or weeks Bypasses complex schemas. Normalizes data directly from Snowflake or Databricks databases without duplication.
  • ✓
    Sub-second POS triggers Deploy automated campaigns instantly when regional shelf velocity falls or competitor spend increases.

Warehouse-native architecture is more secure

Kana operates on a zero-copy data paradigm. We don't store your customer data or POS logs. We simply query directly from your existing data warehouse where it remains under your strict administrative control.

  • SOC 2 Type II certified — fully audited governance and data access controls.
